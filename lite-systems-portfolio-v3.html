01 SYSTEM v163

St. Mark's Management System

Role-based school portal · Node.js / Express

A full portal for St. Mark's High School — student, teacher, parent and admin dashboards over a shared, permissioned data layer, built up through more than 160 iterative releases.

  • Gradebook, assignments with submissions, forum threads and a staff directory
  • Seven-language interface with AI-assisted content translation
  • Security hardening — bcrypt, JWT, rate limiting, Helmet CSP, authenticated data endpoints
  • Local AI monitor with automatic model switching over Ollama

02 SYSTEM v53

TradeTrack

Business management platform · Express / PostgreSQL

A day-to-day operations platform for small businesses — point of sale, inventory, and finance in one place, with multi-currency support built in.

  • POS, inventory, purchase orders with GRN matching, stock takes and reorder rules
  • CRM with loyalty tiers, recurring invoices and expenses
  • AI insights — cash-flow forecasting, anomaly detection, receipt OCR
  • Isolated Owner Console with its own auth, audit log and force-logout
